# CLAUDE.md — GottFA1_PLuS

Guidance for AI assistants working in this repository.

## What this is

VHDL emulation of the **Gottlieb System 1** pinball MPU for the **GottFA1_PLuS**
DIY board. Toolchain: **Intel Quartus Prime Lite 22.1**. Target FPGA:
**Cyclone 10 LP `10CL006YE144C8G`**. Top-level entity: `SYS1` (`SYS1.vhd`, v9.03).
This is a hardware/FPGA project — there are **no automated tests**; "build" means a
Quartus compilation.

## Directory roles

- `GottFA1_PLuS_HW21x_Cyclone10/` — the Quartus project: `SYS1.qpf` (project),
  `SYS1.qsf` (assignments/pinout), `SYS1.sdc` (timing), `SYS1.vhd` (top), plus
  `SYS1_assignment_defaults.qdf` and `output_file.cof`.
- `lib_common/` — shared VHDL cores & peripherals: PPS/4-2 CPU, T65 core,
  RIOT R6530, display/switch/solenoid logic, SD card, EEPROM, DFPlayer sound.
- `lib_cyclone_10/` — Cyclone-10 specific Intel IP (ROM/RAM megafunctions:
  `*.qip` + `*.vhd` + `*.cmp`).

## Important constraints

- **Relative paths:** `SYS1.qsf` references libraries as `../lib_common/...` and
  `../lib_cyclone_10/...`. The three directories must remain siblings — do not move
  or rename them, and keep the repo root as their common parent.
- **Don't commit build artifacts:** `db/`, `incremental_db/`, `greybox_tmp/`,
  `atom_netlists/`, `output_files/`, `*.qws` are generated by Quartus and are
  excluded via `.gitignore`. Backup/old variants (`*.bak`, `* - Kopie.*`,
  `*_old.vhd`, `*_new.vhd`, `archive/`) are excluded too.
- **Mono-repo growth:** this repo will gain further hardware/FPGA variants as new
  sibling directories. To track a new one, add it to the whitelist at the top of
  `.gitignore`.
